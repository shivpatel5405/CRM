Everything Your Sales Team Needs to Win

Eliminate administrative friction. Focus on closing deals and building high-value customer relationships.

7-Stage Visual Kanban

Track prospects seamlessly across New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, and Lost stages with instant pipeline velocity metrics.

1-Click Lead Conversion

Atomic database transactions convert won prospects directly into active Customer records and linked Primary Contacts without duplicate data entry.

Customer 360° Profile

Consolidate company details, multi-contact stakeholders, chronological meeting notes, opportunities, and scheduled activities in a single view.

Opportunity Valuations

Track expected close dates, win probabilities (0–100%), and calculate real-time weighted pipeline valuation alongside closed-won revenues.

Production REST API

Complete Django REST Framework API layer with browsable documentation, session/basic authentication, pagination, and multi-field filters.
